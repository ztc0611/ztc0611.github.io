Aweigh
Live departures for Washington State Ferries, on iPhone, iPhone Duo and Apple Watch.

ABOUT
Aweigh is a departure board for Washington State Ferries, on iPhone, iPhone Duo and Apple Watch. Choose a route and a direction to see the next sailings with delay-aware times, cancellations, drive-up space and reservable space. Follow every boat on a live fleet map, check terminal cameras before you leave, and read the alerts for your route. On Apple Watch, favorite routes count down to the next sailing and show the vehicle space left. On iPhone Duo, your routes stay beside a full departure board.
