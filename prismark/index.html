Prismark

Anything you’d read, read to you.

Save an article from the web, open a PDF or a book, and press play. Prismark reads it aloud and marks each sentence as it goes, so you never lose your place.

Download on the App Store

For iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo. Requires iOS 26 or later.

Natural voices. No account. Works offline.

Choose a voice and star your favorites. The voices run on your device, so there’s nothing to sign up for.

Save it now. Hear it later.

Share an article from your web browser, and it arrives cleaned up and ready to play. Paste text or a link, or open a PDF or a document from Files. Sort it all into folders, each with a marker color of its own.

Books, by chapter.

Jump between chapters in longer books, or tap any sentence to start from there.

Close it. Keep listening.

Prismark for iPhone Duo opens like a book: the text on one page and the player on the other. Close it and keep listening on the cover. On iPhone, lock the screen and it keeps reading.

Fast, and still clear.

Turn the dial to set the speed, and listen at the pace that suits you.

Your color, down to the icon.

Pick the color that marks everything else, the app icon included.
