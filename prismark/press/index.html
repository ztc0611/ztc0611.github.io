Description

Prismark is a listening app for iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo. Save an article from the web, open a PDF or a book, and press play. Prismark reads it aloud with natural voices that run on the device, and marks each sentence as it goes, so you never lose your place. Tap any sentence to jump to it, move between chapters in longer books, and keep listening with the screen locked. On iPhone Duo it opens like a book, with the text on one page and the player on the other, and keeps playing on the cover when you close it.
