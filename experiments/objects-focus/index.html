Pixellate Camera

Less color.
More character.

A live camera that shoots like the 1998 Game Boy Camera: four colors, heavy dither, full metadata, and GPS.

The app’s three-pass dithering engine blends error diffusion with a Bayer matrix, keeping faces recognizable while textures go coarse.

Try the camera on the mat. The slider changes the image; the shutter keeps a print of your chosen mix.

Printing the Dream

A patient little
drawing machine.

Imports an image into Tomodachi Life on Switch 1 or 2, without modifying the console.

A desktop converter finds efficient brush and paint-bucket passes. A Raspberry Pi Pico redraws the image by acting as a wired controller over USB.
