Pixellate Camera

iOS, on the App Store since 2025

A live camera that shoots like the 1998 Game Boy Camera: four colors, heavy dither, full metadata, and GPS.

Every viewfinder frame runs through a three pass dithering engine that blends Floyd-Steinberg error diffusion with a Bayer matrix, so faces stay recognizable while textures go fully retro. Palettes are editable down to the hex value.

Printing the Dream

Hardware and software, open source, 2026

Imports any image into Tomodachi Life on Switch 1 or 2, without modifying the console.

A desktop converter reduces a PNG to the game's palette, chooses the cheapest brush and paint bucket passes, and minimizes cursor travel. A Raspberry Pi Pico then redraws it by impersonating a wired controller over USB. The slide on the table is a photograph of that rig mid draw.

Aweigh

iOS and watchOS, awaiting App Review

A rider first iPhone and Apple Watch app for the largest ferry system in the US, with live departures presented as a split flap board.

WSDOT already publishes much of this data. Aweigh's contribution is the arrangement: schedules, vessel positions, capacity, cameras, and alerts organized around the trip a rider is actually trying to make, rather than around the shape of the source feeds.
