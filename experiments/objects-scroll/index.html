Aweigh

The next boat.
A glance away.

Ferry travel comes with questions. Is the boat on time? Is there room? Which terminal camera should I check?

Aweigh brings schedules, vessel locations, capacity, cameras, and alerts together around the trip a rider is trying to make.

Pixellate
Camera

Less detail.
More character.

A live camera with the spirit of the 1998 Game Boy Camera. Four colors, heavy dither, and all the metadata and GPS you'd expect today.

A custom three-pass engine blends error diffusion and a Bayer matrix, keeping faces recognizable while textures go wonderfully coarse.

Printing
the Dream

A very patient
pair of hands.

Bring an image into Tomodachi Life on Switch 1 or 2, without modifying the console.

A desktop converter picks a palette, plans brush and paint-bucket passes, and reduces cursor travel. A Raspberry Pi Pico takes it from there, acting as a wired controller and drawing the picture.
