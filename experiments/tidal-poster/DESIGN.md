# A little further out

The user's earlier mountains.html had atmosphere, landscape layers and occasional surprises. This takes that idea into a real three-dimensional coastal scene, informed by the ferry work in the portfolio.

A shore-level view opens across blue-green water, with nearby rocks, forested headlands, distant mountains, and a small ferry. Reflective water and changing light carry the visual interest. The landscape occupies the full opening; quiet, fixed typography identifies the person. The work remains in normal document flow below.

Palette: deep water #163548, forest #304b43, sky #8aadb7, warm sunlight #efd4a2, pale type #f4f6ec, portfolio paper #f4f6f5. Scene colors change continuously with time. Typography uses Helvetica Neue for consistent local rendering, readable fixed navigation and large regular-weight name.

Initial lighting follows the visitor's local clock without geolocation. A time slider offers a deliberate interaction, with a direct return to local time. Solar altitude follows the real sun at 48.54193° N, 122.83040° W, with seasonal daylight and civil twilight. Horizontal sun placement stays composed for this view. Controls occupy fixed geometry throughout loading and changing labels. Scene availability reveals them without shifting the composition.

The readability boundary is structural: the expressive scene is the first viewport; portfolio navigation and project content are ordinary accessible HTML. No forced tour, scroll capture, custom cursor or hidden project navigation.

Arrival begins in a matching veil of fog that clears over the already-rendered landscape. Local date chooses a stable imagined atmosphere, with a manual rain toggle. Rain arrives gradually through near-total overcast, darker wet surfaces, falling streaks and small expanding impact rings in the water. Deliberate clicks create larger rings; moving across the composition only eases the camera.

The trees have tapered wooden trunks embedded below the terrain and irregular drooping branch volumes around fuller shaded crowns. The filled interiors let the modest tree count read as a forest without copying real-world planting density. Fine bark and foliage patterns, moss and mineral grain add surface detail without photographic assets. Distant firs use fewer branches; shared instanced geometry keeps the forest economical.

The small ferry borrows its double-ended silhouette, twin wheelhouses, dark hull, green belt and open car-deck ends from the supplied ferry references. Warm window rows and mast lights keep it legible after dark.

Rain lighting shifts toward slate gray: a low, nearly closed cloud ceiling, muted blue-green water, neutral diffuse light and mist through the headlands. Forest green remains visible, while the ferry windows glow warmer even during a rainy afternoon. The same weather blend eases every component together.

The supplied mist photograph guides broken bands threading through the canopy, with uneven wisps rather than isolated rounded clouds. Several banks sit at different depths so foreground trees remain crisp against mist covering the slopes behind them. Rain gradually lowers the banks into the hills, and clearing weather removes them.

The headlands themselves form low bedrock cliffs and broad shelves, reflecting the continuous rocky coast in the supplied photographs. Gray stone rises from the water into green and dry golden grass, with trees rooted in the vegetated areas.

Dry grass uses an independent, irregular spatial pattern, so yellow patches do not repeat the terrain ridges. Haze rises into the rainy sky and uses the terrain fog color after the same output conversion, removing the dark sky cutout behind fogged mountains.

The sky cloud field uses gradient noise with warped coordinates to avoid squared-off shapes, and low-cloud particles feather at every edge.

The base water swell also uses three drifting noise fields with different scales and orientations. This removes the repeating bands from clear weather as well as rain, while preserving deliberate circular click ripples.

The portfolio continues as one complete page: featured apps, the three smaller visual projects, Godot contributions with optional technical detail, then compact rows for further tools and experiments. Helvetica Neue and the established left-aligned type scale stay consistent. Wide layouts pair contribution titles with their explanation; narrow layouts stack them in reading order. The scene remains the expressive centerpiece.

The document palette follows the system appearance independently of scene time: paper #f4f6f5, ink #233c42, slate #13252b, pale text #e3ece9, muted text #a2b7bb and separators #385158. Dark mode changes the page surfaces and typography while reusing the same project images. A footer appearance selector permits an explicit preference. Links with a large arrow use one continuous underline beneath the whole link, not separate typographic underlines.
